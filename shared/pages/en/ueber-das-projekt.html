About Learning Courses

Education and knowledge should be accessible to everyone: people preparing for university, current students, those returning to a subject, and anyone interested in learning for its own sake. No particular qualification is required.

Learning Courses provides structured learning materials with many lessons in mathematics and computer science. You can follow the lessons in sequence or look up specific topics. Its dedicated reader supports notes, bookmarks, favorites and personal reading progress stored in your browser. The materials can also be read offline.

This is an independent educational resource. The materials help readers get started, review and deepen their knowledge, but do not replace formal teaching, study or recognized qualifications.

Errors or simplifications may occur; feedback on specific passages is welcome.

Contact

Please send feedback, suggestions or error reports to info@monowlith.com or use GitHub Issues in the repository of the relevant course. Include the course, module, lesson, section where applicable, and the relevant passage.
