Usage notes and methodology

The learning materials are intended for personal education, deeper study and orientation. They do not award qualifications and cannot replace classes or university courses.

Quality

The materials were created with AI assistance and compiled editorially. Automated checks verify technical completeness and links, but do not replace a comprehensive external subject-matter review. Errors, outdated information and misleading simplifications may remain.

Classification

The designation of school-level or university-level courses reflects a didactic structure, not an official accreditation.

Corrections

Please report errors with a precise reference to info@monowlith.com or use GitHub Issues in the repository of the relevant course.
