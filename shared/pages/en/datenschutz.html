Reader data stored locally

The reader stores notes, favorites, bookmarks, reading progress and display preferences in your browser so they are still available when you return. No account or transmission of these contents to the publisher is needed for this. You can delete these records in your browser or export a backup yourself. Section 25 of the German TDDDG applies to storing and accessing information on a device. Its exemption for strictly necessary functions depends on an expressly requested service.

No account is required for reading or for these local functions. The current reader does not automatically transmit personal notes, favorites or reading progress to the publisher or GitHub. Browser synchronization and extensions may operate independently.

Hosting via GitHub Pages

When the online version is accessed through GitHub Pages, technical connection data are processed by the hosting provider. GitHub describes processing IP addresses for security purposes, in particular. This is separate from locally stored reading records.

Further information: GitHub privacy statement ↗ (external link) and GitHub Pages ↗ (external link).

Email contact

When someone contacts us by email, the contact details and message they provide are used to process and respond to the request.

External links

The reader links to external sources and providers. Opening those links actively may subject visitors to the privacy policies of the destination sites.

Rights and further information

Data subjects can contact info@monowlith.com with privacy-related requests. Their statutory rights under the General Data Protection Regulation apply, including the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.
